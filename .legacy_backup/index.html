    Questions

    Frequently Asked Questions Taxi Medical

    The answers we give on the phone, written down.

    How Much is a Taxi Medical?

    Our medicals are currently priced at a very low rate of £60.

    What's Involved in the Taxi Medical?

    Our examination will vary between each Local Authority as they decide what questions and tests are involved in the medical. But all medicals include an eye test, and a blood pressure test.

    Every medical must follow the same guidelines that a driver must reach Group 2 Medical Standards. You can find your medical form online and see what’s involved by visiting your Local Authority’s website and searching for ‘Taxi Licensing’.

    How do I Request My Medical Records?

    Most GP Surgeries are able to provide your Medical Summary Care Records on the same day and Full Medical Records within 28 days. This service is for Free, GP Surgeries (under GDPR / SAR) are not allowed to charge for this request.

    Please Contact your surgery to obtain the relevant records for your medical. Some Surgeries require written authority for such requests. Sample letters that you can download and use for this request are in the What You Need to Bring section on this page.

    How Do I Book My Taxi Medical?
    • Pay over the phone when you secure your booking.
    • Pay on the day is available (Please ask when booking. Cash Payments on the day are accepted at most clinics).
    Can I Download my Taxi Medical Form?

    Most medical forms are available online from your local council website. We do keep PDF Copies of most Taxi Medical forms for clients to download. Please note the copies we store may not be uptodate.

    We do try our best to keep the latest version of the Taxi Medical Form on our website, However Direct Taxi Medicals or its affiliates do not hold any responsibility if the incorrect form is supplied by us. The onus is on the customer to provide the Correct Medical Form. Delayed processing of Licensing applications may occur and we cannot be held accountable.

    Who Can Complete My Form Questionnaire

    Most Taxi driver medicals can be completed by our General Practitioner or Doctor. However some Local Authorities state only the driver’s own General Practitioner may do so.

    Please download your medical form from your council website or from our download section above and Confirm with your Council that any GP/Doctor can complete your medical before booking.

    Which Councils Do You Cover?

    We are approved to carry out Medicals for most Councils. If your council is not listed please get in touch to discuss. Please obtain the required medical form and confirm with your Local Authority that any GP or Doctor can complete your medical before booking.

    I've Been Banned From Driving and Need a Medical

    For anyone who has been banned from driving and wishes to re-apply for their driving license, You may need a HRO Medical (High Risk Offender) medical such as a drink driver medical or drug driver medical. Please request the DVLA to appoint Direct Taxi Medicals London EC4V 4BE (if this is within your locality). We offer appointments weekly. We are operating as normal for HRO Medicals in line with DVLA GUIDANCE.
