Taxi driver Medical Near Me Local Council Form

Filling in the Near Me Council's specific medical form is crucial when applying for or renewing your taxi driver’s licence. This form ensures that your medical assessment aligns with the local regulatory requirements and health standards set by the council. Each local authority, including Near Me, has unique criteria to guarantee that drivers are fit to operate passenger vehicles safely.

The form helps the medical examiner focus on the aspects most pertinent to the demands of taxi driving near you, such as vision and cardiovascular health, which are essential for public safety.

What You Need to Know About Your Taxi Medical Near You

Our taxi driver medicals are fully approved by Near Me Council. Book your appointment today to get started.

If you're applying for or renewing a private hire driving licence with Near Me Council, a medical assessment is a mandatory part of the process. Taxi Medical is here to simplify that journey. We offer specialised Near Me private hire medicals, guiding you through the entire assessment and providing you with the required certification.

At Taxi Medical, your medical will typically include:

Eye Test: Ensuring your vision meets the required standards for safe driving. Blood Pressure Check: Assessing your cardiovascular health to ensure it's within safe limits. General Health Evaluation: Reviewing your overall health and medical history to determine your fitness to drive.

What's Involved in the Taxi Medical?

Our examination will vary between each Local Authority as they decide what questions and tests are involved in the medical. But all medicals include an eye test, and a blood pressure test.

Whast Does the Taxi Medical Assessment Include?

Every medical must follow the same guidelines that a driver must reach Group 2 Medical Standards. You can find your medical form online and see what’s involved by visiting your Local Authority’s website and searching for ‘Taxi Licensing’.

How do I Request My Medical Records?

Most GP Surgeries are able to provide your Medical Summary Care Records on the same day and Full Medical Records within 28 days. This service is for Free, GP Surgeries (under GDPR / SAR) are not allowed to charge for this request. Please Contact your surgery to obtain the relevant records for your medical.
